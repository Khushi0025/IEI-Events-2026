Strategic Purpose

WHY WE ORGANIZE CAMPUS HACK

Approved by the Head of Department, IEI Campus Hack is engineered to elevate students beyond regular curriculum boundaries and build nationally competitive problem-solvers.

⚡

Technical & Creative Synergy

Encouraging active participation across both hardcore technical stacks and creative expression—bridging the gap between software development and design thinking.

Multidisciplinary
🎯

Practical Problem Solving

Challenging students with real-world industry problem statements, algorithmic thinking, and rapid prototyping under high-energy time constraints.

Skill-Oriented
🇮🇳

College-Level Mentorship & Scouting

Identifying campus-wide standout engineering teams and providing dedicated faculty and senior mentorship to build on their skills and represent the institution at college-level competitions.

Campus Gateway
🌐

Campus Innovation Culture

Building an inclusive, lively, and forward-thinking campus community where seniors and juniors collaborate freely, brainstorm, and build together.

Ecosystem Building
The 3-Day Roadmap

EVENT SCHEDULE & VENUES

Three intense days structured with morning and afternoon sessions, curated for intellectual clash, creative exploration, and full-stack software development.

Stage 01
Day 1
9th • Inauguration Day

Quiz Competition & Debate Championship

📍 Venue: Conference Hall ⏰ Session 1: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM 🥪 Lunch Break: 2:00 PM – 2:30 PM ⏰ Session 2: 2:30 PM – 5:00 PM

Testing core technical knowledge, rapid-fire logical thinking, and articulation. Students engage in an intense debate battleground on emerging technological and societal dilemmas, followed by an audiovisual technical quiz.

Stage 02
Day 2
10th • Creative Sprint

Video Editing & Online Treasure Hunt

📍 Venue: Computer Lab ⏰ Session 1: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM 🥪 Lunch Break: 2:00 PM – 2:30 PM ⏰ Session 2: 2:30 PM – 5:00 PM

Putting creative visual storytelling and cryptographic clue-solving to the test. Digital creators edit cinematic sequences while cyberspace detectives solve cyber puzzles, web riddles, and logic locks in the Online Treasure Hunt.

Grand Finale
Day 3
11th • Hackathon Day

The Full-Fledged Campus Hackathon

📍 Venue: High-Speed Computer Lab 🎓 Official Class Attendance Concession for All Years ⏰ 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM Non-stop

The ultimate coding sprint open to students from all college years (1st, 2nd, 3rd & 4th year). Teams build software or hardware solutions from scratch, guided by on-ground mentors, with dedicated refreshment breaks and on-the-spot jury evaluations.

Department Support

FACILITIES & INFRASTRUCTURE

Fully backed by the college department with seamless technical setups to ensure an uninterrupted, high-performance hacking environment.

⚡

Tech & Network Setup

  • High-speed wired LAN & high-density Wi-Fi connectivity throughout.
  • Uninterrupted power backups & extension board grids for every team.
  • Dedicated computer labs equipped with development environments.
🎙️

Presentation & Staging

  • Conference Hall display projector and high-definition screen setup.
  • Professional sound system with cordless microphones for debates & quiz.
  • Whiteboards & scoring consoles for live, transparent judging.
🍕

Student Care & Refreshments

  • Lunch arrangements for participants, mentors, and jury members.
  • Mid-session energy snacks & beverages during intensive Day 3 hack sprints.
  • Official IEI Coordinator Jerseys for instant on-ground support.
