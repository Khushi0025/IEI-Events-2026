ABOUT IEI CAMPUS HACK

Institution's Innovation Council (IEI) • Fostering Innovation & Excellence

Igniting Innovation, Engineering the Future

IEI Campus Hack is a premier three-day technical extravaganza organized by the coordinators of the Institution's Innovation Council (IEI). Designed to bridge the gap between creative ideation and technical execution, the event brings students together across multiple engineering and creative domains.

Our mission is to build robust problem-solving skills, cultivate an active tech culture on campus, and identify outstanding student talent to be groomed for national-level arenas such as the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).

🎯
Technical & Creative Fusion

5 curated competitions ranging from Debates and Quizzes to Video Editing and a full-fledged Hackathon.

🇮🇳
Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Pipeline

Top performing teams receive dedicated mentorship and grooming for national-level hackathons.

🎓
Open to All College Years

Students from 1st to 4th year are encouraged to participate, backed by official academic attendance leave.
